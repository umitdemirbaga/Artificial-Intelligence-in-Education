# Educational interpretation and responsible use

The companion materials demonstrate methods that may inform teaching, assessment, and research. Their outputs are **not autonomous educational decisions**.

- **Student performance and risk:** Predictions are imperfect and context dependent. False positives can lead to unnecessary intervention or labelling; false negatives can miss support needs.
- **Learner profiling:** Clusters are exploratory patterns, not stable learner types or evidence of fixed learning styles.
- **Probabilistic modelling:** Graph edges are conditional dependencies unless additional identification assumptions and evidence establish causality.
- **Text and well-being:** Sentiment labels are not psychological diagnoses. The Chapter 6 source dataset is not a documented representative sample of students.
- **Dialogue and tutoring:** Simulated conversations and text-based proxies do not establish student learning, emotional recovery, or classroom impact.
- **Classroom vision:** Detected visible actions are not direct measures of cognitive engagement, motivation, or student ability. Follow privacy, consent, and institutional policy requirements.
- **Formative assessment:** Arithmetic checks verify the submitted computation, not the appropriateness of the chosen operation. The Chapter 9 system requires **teacher review** and does not automatically send generated feedback to learners.

Do not upload identifiable student data, clinical records, confidential assessment materials, or private institutional datasets. Use ethically and legally obtained data, document permissions, and obtain necessary institutional approvals before deploying any intervention.
